Today is a special day for me. Of course because it's my birthdayyy. I got wishes from lovely people. I am really so happy.

The weather, the mood, the time, everything feels so nice and perfect. I want to dance in the jungle in rainy and smoky weather and talk to birds. It feels like a dreamy day to me.

I can't explain how nice the winds are coming from my window. When I look outside the window, I see greenery, birds chirping. My father is putting a status on WhatsApp. That's so lovely. He is one of the people who care the most.

My mood is dancing like it's floating in poetry, fiction and more. I want to write poetry, stories that lead to a fictional world, want to watch anime, play a flute, write code, design my world, love everyone. Ahhh so fun.

I had written lessons of the year yesterday:

1. Knowledge is one kind of compression of information.
2. Developing the methods of thinking, thinking abstractly, and doing metacognition are the deepest layers of thinking.
3. Kindness, thoughts, breathing, information inputs live simultaneously and affect each other.
4. Grey areas are mostly true in the real world. "You can change yourself and people". "You cannot change yourself and people". Both statements are true.
5. Getting 1% less wrong is better than punishing yourself for not being good enough.
6. One great aim or one intense positive passion can override almost anything negative.
7. Reading and writing are superpowers.

Anyway, the day is asking me to love myself, and grow every day however small the increment. Always try to be water: still, beautiful, tasteless but important, formless.
