I just came from a walk. It was so good outside - bird chirping, wind, smell of new flowers and leaves, all was lovely. I'd love to spend more time, but it's my exam tomorrow. Sad, nahh!  
I was thinking about myself. I realized, sometimes, I hate people. I just hate without any single reason. Maybe because I get jealous, maybe becase of that person's look, confidence or whatever it is which I don't posses or maybe I own, but don't like someone else side.
  
I try my best to not hate anyone. But I'm a human, have some traits that are unintentional. They just come.  
Anyway, I have more control over these nuanced emotions than ever. Thanks to my diary. Whenever I feel something bad, I talk with it, and decide better.
