# Spanda: My Notes on Intelligence

Human intelligence is not a search engine that searches, ranks and then returns. For eg, when I think about a dog, my brain does not go for a search, but it just comes up. It's like a magnet. It does not move, but the iron comes to it. So, the context (query, goal, or anything) is the magnet. The right knowledge or experience comes to it automatically.  I'd say, cognition is dominated by resonance-constrained activation rather than explicit symbolic retrieval.

And it's not a list of static facts or words either. When I think about dog, I don't get a dictionary definition. I get a cloud (in Jeff Hawkins term "model"). And which part of the cloud lights up depends on what I was thinking about. Dog food context gives me food stuff, not types of dogs breed. Dog behavior context gives me behavior stuff. Same dog model in my brain, but different outputs.  

Brain stores something deeper than words. I never store words. Words come out when I need to express. But inside, it's meaning. Pure meaning. I don't know what it looks like exactly but I feel it's not language either.  

Here, I call the model "Spanda" that stores experience and knowledge about a specific thing. Haha. I know, "Spanda" might sound strange. But it's a Sanskrit word, meaning vibration, dynamics. 

And one Spanda is sometimes not enough. When I think about natural numbers, (1, 2, 3, 4,....), they come in sequence not because I searched but because one activates the next. Context was too big for one knowledge unit. So multiple come, one after another. Like dominoes. Another example, suppose you have a talk on branches of science, your brain lets physics Spanda comes up, then probably biology, and then chemistry. Look, one spanda is not enough here. In fact, there would be Spandas in hirarechical form, Physics spanda may contain more spandas for different topics and context.   

The brain is not infinite physically. If bigger AI needs bigger GPU, we are doing something fundamentally wrong. The brain forgets. It decays information. But it keeps something deep, capability, not just facts. I forgot most of my childhood. But I think better because of it. I perform better. The experience compressed into ability. That's the real storage.  

So intelligence is not about storing more. It's about compressing better. Every experience should make the structure stronger, not the storage bigger. The measure of intelligence is how efficiently you compress experience into reusable structure.  

A knowledge unit, I call Spanda, is not a static pair of elements and weights. That was wrong. It's a function over all possible meanings. Most meanings are near zero. Some are high. Context collapses it to the relevant region instantly, like how saying "numbers between 2 and 5" immediately gives you a range without searching the entire number line. 

And every Spanda has a lifecycle. When it's born it's full of detail, weak in structure. As it gets used, details decay but structure gets stronger. When it's mature it's sparse, compressed, deeply connected. Eventually it might crystallize into pure capability.  

Spanda is also not fixed. It connects with other Spandas. When bicycle meets transport they at the boundary and something passes between them, The core of each stays the same. The bicycle is still bicycle, but the boundary flexes. We can imagine water droplets of different colors (just imagine) that touch another droplets, they keep their identity but share something at the interface.

Context is not just another knowledge unit. That was my first mistake in the previous draft. Context is a commander. It has a goal. It broadcasts into the space of all Spandas and the right ones wake up, not because they were found but because they resonate with the context. Dormant Spandas wake up. Silent ones can't be reached by natural context anymore, their encoding has degraded. Unavailable ones are gone. (Encoding meaning making spandas talk with each other, in the sense of how we use language for communication, we convert our feelings, ideas in words and then convert into sound waves. Similarly, encoding spandas means making it understandable by context and other spandas).

Additionally, I noticed brain always want conclusion. It means its curiosity leads it to find the conclusion. One example, is the universe infinite or finite. We don't know yet, but we can't stay without conclusion. In other word, Spandas don't just store what was experienced. They form **empty places** (gaps) that get filled by generalization and imagination. Intelligence is not just compression of the past. It's **projection into the unknown** using the structure of what is already known.

I don't know everything yet. I don't know exactly what passes between two Spandas when they connect. I don't know how to write the compression mathematically. I don't know what the internal structure of a Spanda looks like when it has relationships inside it, not just intensities.

But I know this:
*The brain is not a database. It's not a search engine. It's not a bigger and bigger pile of parameters.*

It's something that takes experience and makes it smaller, deeper, more connected. And somehow from that understanding comes. Instantly. Without search. Like iron coming to a magnet.

That's what I'm trying to build.
