One thing I must improve in myself: whenever I learn something or read books, I start visualizing myself teaching those lessons to others. But I shouldn't, at least in this phase. Instead I should visualize the lesson and its applications on my own life.
Why should I even care about others, especially in this learning journey. Another reason is it wastes my mental energy and it sarts to seem - that lesson is for them, not for me. That's why, I forget to implement in my own life.
