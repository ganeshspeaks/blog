title: "Before Me"
tags: ["poem"]
---

There were two paths before me.  
One promised sweet fruit,  
the other was heading towards bitter truth.  

They asked me, "why choose the good?"  
Yet my heart whispered, "why not the bad?"  

Why must life be only about good days,  
why not welcome pain?  

Then I remembered,  
the river never asks whether the water brings life or destruction.  

The moment that stayed with me was neither joy or devastation.  
The thing that asked nothing of me lasted long enough.  

The fish does not stop the current.  
And perhaps, so should I?
